1. AI-Powered Travel Planning (The Core Hook)
Constraint-Based Itinerary Generator: Users input travel dates, budget, group size, and interests (e.g., wildlife, waterfalls, culture). The AI outputs a day-by-day, geographically logical plan.
Twin-State Logistics Engine: The AI automatically factors in realistic inter-state travel times (e.g., Guwahati to Shillong Sumo taxi timings, Nimati Ghat ferry schedules to Majuli).
Dynamic Seasonal Alerts: Real-time or rule-based warnings (e.g., "Kaziranga is closed May–Oct," "Dawki river is best visited Nov–Mar," "Avoid Jorhat during Rongali Bihu due to price surges").

2. Smart Aggregation & Discovery (Hotels & Transport)
Curated Listings with Metadata: Displays name, location, distance from key attractions, and estimated price ranges.
Aggregated Ratings: Pulls live ratings from Google Places API, Booking.com, or Agoda (e.g., "4.5★ on Google").
Affiliate Redirection: "Check Best Price" buttons that open partner websites (Agoda, Booking.com, RedBus) in a new tab with pre-filled search parameters (earning you affiliate commission).
"Unlisted" Local Inventory: A manually curated database of grassroots homestays, local taxi unions, and independent boatmen not found on major OTAs, featuring direct "Inquire via WhatsApp" buttons.

3. Destination Deep-Dive Pages (Reality-Checked Insights)
Pros & Cons: Honest, ground-level assessments (e.g., Pro: Stunning living root bridges. Con: Steep, slippery stairs; not wheelchair accessible.)
Cultural Do’s & Don’ts: Specific ethical guidelines (e.g., "Ask permission before photographing in Mishing villages," "Strict no-plastic zones in Mawlynnong").
Location Insights: Embedded maps, nearest medical facilities, network connectivity status (e.g., "Jio works best here, Airtel is patchy"), and local emergency numbers.
Must-Try Local Experiences: Curated lists of hyper-local food, hidden viewpoints, and cultural events.

4. Local Marketplace (Discovery & Direct-Connect Only)
Artisan & Producer Profiles: Story-driven profiles of local creators (e.g., Sualkuchi silk weavers, Lakadong turmeric farmers, Khasi bamboo crafters) with high-quality photos.
Experience Hosting: Listings for unique activities (e.g., "Mishing village cooking class," "Private sunrise Brahmaputra boat ride," "Guided Dawki trek with a local Khasi youth").
"Bibek Verified" Badge: A trust indicator showing the Bibek team has manually verified the legitimacy and safety of the seller/host.
Direct Connection CTAs: No shopping cart. Buttons exclusively for "Contact on WhatsApp," "View Instagram," or "Email Host."

5. Travel Buddy Matcher (Niche Community)
Trip Posting: Users can create posts detailing their plan (e.g., "Female traveler seeking buddy for 2-day Majuli trip, Nov 12-14, Budget: ₹3k").
Smart Filtering: Match users based on destination, travel dates, budget tier, and travel style (e.g., backpacker, luxury, photography-focused).
Secure Contact Reveal: In-app messaging or a system that only reveals phone numbers after mutual consent.
Safety First: Prominent disclaimers ("Meet in public places," "Share itinerary with family") and a "Report User" button.

6. Vendor / Partner Portal (Lightweight)
Self-Service Profile Management: A simple dashboard for local guides, homestay owners, and artisans to upload photos, update their WhatsApp number, and edit their descriptions.
Inquiry Dashboard: A simple log showing how many times users clicked "Contact via WhatsApp" on their profile (to prove the platform's value to them).

7. Admin & Operational Dashboard (For the Bibek Team)
Content Management System (CMS): To manually add, edit, or approve "Unlisted" inventory, marketplace profiles, and Deep-Dive destination pages.
Verification Workflow: A system to review and approve "Bibek Verified" badges for vendors (e.g., uploading a photo of their tourism ID or Aadhaar for safety checks).
Affiliate Link Manager: To easily update and track Booking.com, Agoda, or RedBus affiliate URLs.
Community Moderation: Tools to review and remove spam or unsafe posts in the Travel Buddy section.
