# Travel Local

**An AI-powered travel planning and local discovery platform for Northeast India.**

Travel Local helps travelers plan trips, discover hidden destinations, find local accommodation and transportation, and connect directly with local communities. By combining AI-powered itineraries with authentic local experiences, we aim to make traveling across Northeast India easier, more reliable, and more rewarding for both visitors and local businesses.

## The problem

Planning a trip across Northeast India can be challenging. Information about destinations, transportation, accommodation, seasonal accessibility, and local experiences is scattered across multiple platforms.

Travelers often struggle to create realistic itineraries that account for travel times, budgets, road conditions, and local transport schedules. Meanwhile, many homestays, independent guides, artisans, and small businesses remain difficult to discover online.

This creates a gap between travelers looking for authentic experiences and local communities that could benefit from tourism.

## Our solution

Travel Local brings travel planning, destination discovery, and local connections together in one platform.

* **AI-powered planning:** Generate personalized, day-by-day itineraries based on budget, travel dates, group size, interests, and realistic travel constraints.
* **Smart discovery:** Explore accommodation, transportation, and local services with relevant information, estimated prices, and booking links.
* **Destination guides:** Access practical insights about accessibility, seasonal conditions, local customs, connectivity, safety, and hidden attractions.
* **Local marketplace:** Discover homestays, artisans, local guides, traditional producers, and community-hosted experiences.
* **Travel Buddy Matcher:** Find compatible travel companions based on destinations, dates, budgets, and travel preferences.
* **Direct local connections:** Contact local providers through WhatsApp and other supported channels without requiring an integrated marketplace checkout.

## Who it serves

* **Travelers:** Solo travelers, backpackers, families, groups, and adventure enthusiasts.
* **Local businesses:** Homestay owners, taxi operators, boatmen, guides, and tourism service providers.
* **Artisans and producers:** Weavers, craftspeople, farmers, and small local businesses.
* **Local communities:** Communities seeking greater visibility and economic opportunities through responsible tourism.

## What makes Travel Local different

### Travel planning built around regional realities

Unlike generic itinerary generators, Travel Local aims to account for the practical challenges of exploring Northeast India, including intercity travel, shared taxis, ferry connections, seasonal accessibility, and realistic journey times.

### Discover beyond mainstream tourism

We want travelers to discover lesser-known destinations, local experiences, independent businesses, and cultural activities that are often overlooked by conventional travel platforms.

### Honest, practical destination information

Our destination guides focus on more than attractions. They also highlight accessibility challenges, cultural etiquette, connectivity, safety considerations, and other information travelers need before visiting.

### Community-first tourism

By connecting travelers directly with local providers, artisans, and experience hosts, Travel Local aims to improve their online visibility and help tourism create opportunities for local communities.

## Initial launch strategy

Travel Local will focus on building a reliable travel experience across selected destinations in Northeast India before expanding its coverage.

1. **Select initial destinations:** Start with a manageable cluster of popular and lesser-known destinations.
2. **Build reliable travel data:** Collect destination information, routes, estimated journey times, seasonal guidance, and accommodation options.
3. **Onboard local providers:** Connect with homestays, guides, transport operators, artisans, and experience hosts.
4. **Launch core features:** Prioritize AI itinerary generation, destination guides, and accommodation and transport discovery.
5. **Test with real travelers:** Evaluate itinerary feasibility, information accuracy, and the usefulness of local listings.
6. **Expand gradually:** Add destinations, providers, and advanced features based on feedback and available data.

## Data accuracy and responsible AI

Travel information can change quickly, especially in remote regions. Travel Local aims to distinguish verified information from estimates and AI-generated suggestions.

* Travel times and prices should be identified as estimates where appropriate.
* Transportation schedules and seasonal restrictions should be verified against reliable sources.
* Provider verification should follow clearly defined criteria.
* Personal information should be handled responsibly, with appropriate privacy controls.
* AI-generated itineraries should be validated against available travel and geographic data wherever possible.

**AI recommendations are planning assistance, not guarantees.** Travelers should confirm bookings, transportation arrangements, and access conditions before departure.
